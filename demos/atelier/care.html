Care

How the pieces are kept

Kundan

Soft cloth. No ultrasonic. Pearls off before perfume.

Silver-tone

A dry pouch. Tarnish cloth twice a season, not polish paste.

Temple gold-tone

Motifs are raised. Store flat. Do not stack bangles on the dori.

Resize

Kadas and neck dori can be adjusted on the floor. Earrings, no.

Returns within 7 days if the seal pouch is unopened. Custom metal changes are final once you leave with the piece.
