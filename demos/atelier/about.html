The house

A floor, a bench, and a person who will change the metal in front of you.

Atelier is the demonstration of a jewellery house site: a front door, a filterable collection, a piece page where the variant changes the price, a bag that survives the next page, and a checkout. It is not a client’s store.

The work it answers is a catalogue-and-billing site. This one keeps the sale inside the browser so you can walk the whole path.
